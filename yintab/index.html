Landmark Projects & Impact

🕊️ The Djibouti Initiative: Designing Peace for Somalia

Prof. Badejo played a primary role in designing, negotiating, and implementing the Djibouti Initiative (2000), which established national governance structures for Somalia after a decade of civil strife. This foundational work earned him the Djibouti National Order of 27 June 1977 in 2025.

As Chief of Staff to H.E. In 2026, President Ramos-Horta conferred the Medal of the Order of Timor-Leste.

About Us

Yintab Strategy Consults

Founded in April 2017 by Prof. Babafemi A. Badejo upon his retirement from nearly 24 years of distinguished United Nations service, YSC brings the rigour, methodology, and strategic insight honed at the highest levels of international peace and governance to African institutions, governments, and civil society.

Our Vision

An Africa where institutions are accountable, conflicts are mediated, and development is sustainable, grounded in the rule of law and respect for human dignity.

Our Mission

To strengthen governance, build peace, advance human rights and humanitarian action, and promote accountability and good governance across and beyond Africa through world-class research, institutional design, and strategic advisory services.

Our Values

  • Integrity: Highest ethical standards in all work
  • Independence: Objective analysis free from political interference
  • Impact: Tangible improvements in governance, peace, human rights, and humanitarian outcomes
  • African Ownership: African solutions to African challenges
  • Excellence: Rigour, evidence, and global best practice
  • Human Dignity: Protection of human life and dignity at the centre

Mr. Bunmi Makinwa

Senior Advisor, Communications & Development Strategy

Bunmi Makinwa is a Nigerian business executive, development specialist, and communications strategist with over two decades of leadership experience at the highest levels of international development, public health, and media.

Read-more

He is the Chief Executive Officer of Auniquei, a private entrepreneurial initiative in communication for leadership, and he brings to Yintab Strategy Consults a rare combination of strategic insight, global operational experience, and a deep commitment to African advancement. His distinguished career includes service as Africa Regional Director of the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA), Director of the UNAIDS New York Office, and Director and Representative of UNAIDS to the African Union Commission in Addis Ababa. Across these roles, he led high-stakes engagements in more than 60 countries, over 40 of them in Africa, shaping policies and programmes on population, health, and sustainable development. He was Nigeria's candidate for the post of Executive Director of UNFPA in 2010. Mr. Makinwa holds a Bachelor of Arts in Modern Languages from the University of Ibadan (1980) and a Master of Arts in Philosophy, with a concentration in Political Philosophy, from the same institution (1985). He earned a Master of Public Administration from the John F. Kennedy School of Government at Harvard University (1993), concentrating in management and policy. He has also studied at institutions in Germany, South Africa, and the United Nations System Staff College. A prolific writer and commentator, Mr. Makinwa is the author and co-author of numerous publications on communications, public health, and development, including Communications Framework for HIV/AIDS: A New Direction and Social Marketing: An Effective Tool in the Global Response to HIV/AIDS. He writes analytical columns and opinion pieces for leading outlets including The Guardian, Daily Trust, Business Day, Premium Times, Sahara Reporters, TheCable, and AllAfrica. He runs the blog Politics, Policies, Poetry and Life in Africa and is a sought-after voice on political leadership, elections, and social change. He serves on the boards of several businesses and not-for-profit organisations, including the ONE Campaign Africa Policy Advisory Board and Social Workers Beyond Borders, and is the current Chair of the Board of the African Population and Health Research Center (APHRC). He is the founder of INCLUDE International, a not-for-profit advocacy and performance analysis organisation. He is a Fellow of the Harvard Institute for International Development and a Grand Commander of the National Order of Benin Republic. At Yintab Strategy Consults, Mr. Makinwa provides strategic advisory support on communications, institutional positioning, and development strategy, strengthening the firm's capacity to translate rigorous analysis into influential public discourse and policy impact.

Dr. Ayodele Patrick Aderinwale, MFR

Senior Advisor, Leadership & Governance

Dr. Ayodele Patrick Aderinwale, MFR, is one of Nigeria's foremost development specialists, governance experts, and business leaders, with a career spanning over three decades at the intersection of leadership development, institutional reform, and enterprise.

Read-more

He serves as an Adviser to Yintab Strategy Consults, bringing to the firm an unparalleled network of continental and national leadership, as well as deep practical experience in governance, capacity building, and organisational transformation. Dr. Aderinwale earned a Bachelor of Science in Political Science from the University of Lagos (1985) and a Master of Science in Political Economy and International Relations from the same institution (1987). He is an alumnus of the United Nations University's International Leadership Academy and Harvard University's Executive Programme for Leaders in Development. He has also participated in the Salzburg Seminar, the Sixth Lisbon Youth Summit, and the Center for Advanced Studies in International Negotiation in Geneva, Switzerland. Dr. Aderinwale served as Executive Director of the Africa Leadership Forum (ALF) in Ota, Ogun State, where he designed and led pioneering programmes including the Regional African Parliamentarians Conference, the Africa Women's Forum, the Legislative Internship Programme, and the Democratic Leadership Training Workshop. He co-created the Conference on Stability, Security, Development and Cooperation in Africa (CSSDCA), which was adopted by the African Union in 2002—a landmark contribution to the continent's peace and governance architecture. He also participated in the initial drafting of the Millennium Plan for Africa (MAP), which later became the New Partnership for Africa's Development (NEPAD), and was an inaugural member of the Nigeria Steering Committee of the African Peer Review Mechanism (APRM). His continental service includes roles as Vice President of the Economic, Social and Cultural Council (ECOSOCC) of the African Union, Chair of the Parliament of the African Capacity Building Foundation (based in Harare, Zimbabwe), and Lead Consultant for the Union Government Project, which drafted the institution of a United States of Africa. He has provided consulting services to the United Nations, the European Union, and the former Organisation of African Unity. In the private sector, Dr. Aderinwale is Chairman of Justrite Limited, one of Nigeria's leading indigenous supermarket chains, which he co-founded with his wife, Mrs. Oluwatosin Aderinwale. He is also Chairman of the Business School Netherlands, Nigeria; Chairman of the Board of Governors of The Bells Educational Services; Chairman of Docu-thrill; and Deputy Chief Coordinator of the Olusegun Obasanjo Presidential Library (OOPL). He has previously served on the Board of Editors of the Daily Times of Nigeria and as a member of the Commonwealth Peoples Forum 2003 Steering Committee. A prolific writer, scholar, and motivational speaker, Dr. Aderinwale's publications include CSSDCA: An African Agenda for Peace and Prosperity (2001), Value, Ideology and Nation Building (2003), and Abebe: Portrait of a Nigerian Leader. A festschrift, Leadership, Ethics and Values: Collection of Essays in Honour of Ayodele Aderinwale, was published in 2012. In recognition of his contributions to national development, he was conferred with the National Honour of Member of the Order of the Federal Republic (MFR) in 2006. The Ayodele Aderinwale Foundation for Education and Leadership in Africa (AAFELA) was established in his honour, providing scholarships to young Nigerians across the country. At Yintab Strategy Consults, Dr. Aderinwale provides high-level advisory support on leadership development, governance reform, and institutional strategy, reinforcing the firm's mission to strengthen African institutions and advance accountable, effective governance.

Ambassador Nureldin M. H. Satti

Senior Advisor, Peace Mediation & Conflict Resolution

Ambassador Nureldin M. H. Satti is a seasoned Sudanese diplomat and a retired senior United Nations official with a career spanning over four decades at the intersection of peace mediation, conflict resolution, and international diplomacy.

Read-more

He brings to Yintab Strategy Consults extensive experience in supporting African peace processes and strengthening continental governance architecture. His distinguished career in the United Nations includes service as Principal Deputy Special Representative of the Secretary-General at the United Nations political and peacekeeping missions to Burundi (UNOB and ONUB) from 2002 to 2006, including Acting SRSG from April to December 2006 . Earlier, he served as Director of the UNESCO Dar es Salaam Cluster Office and Director of UNESCO’s Regional Programme for Education for Emergencies and Reconstruction (PEER) in Nairobi, where he was closely involved in the Somalia Peace Process and served as an observer at the Arusha Peace Process for Burundi and the IGAD process for Sudan . In his diplomatic career, Ambassador Satti served as Ambassador of Sudan to France, Portugal, Switzerland, and the Vatican, as well as Permanent Delegate to UNESCO (1992–1996) . He was appointed Ambassador of Sudan to the United States in July 2020, becoming Khartoum’s first ambassador to Washington in 23 years, before resigning in January 2022 in protest against the military coup in Sudan . He has since been actively involved in civic movements to restore peace and democratic rule in Sudan. Ambassador Satti holds a Master’s degree from the University of Lyon and a Doctorate in Literature from the University of Paris-Sorbonne (1974) . He is a Global Fellow and co-chair of the Sudans Working Group at the Woodrow Wilson Center in Washington, DC, and has served as a Senior Fellow and Distinguished African Scholar at the Center (2008–2009) . He was awarded the National Order of the Légion d’Honneur by the French Republic and the Medal of Saint Gregory by the Vatican. At Yintab Strategy Consults, Ambassador Satti provides high-level advisory support on peace mediation, conflict resolution, and governance reform, strengthening the firm’s capacity to support African institutions in addressing complex peace and security challenges.
