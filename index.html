About Us

Who we are

YINTAB Strategy Consults, the consultancy subsidiary of YINTAB Limited, is an association of diplomats, legal professionals, business owners and academics drawn from different parts of the world, bringing decades of experience and proven methodologies developed and refined in leading international organisations such as the United Nations — with its discipline and integrity ethos — as well as in some of the most difficult political theatres in the world.

YSC brings some of the finest hands in global humanitarian services, public and corporate governance, policy formulation and implementation, legal analysis, secretarial and representation services, mediation and arbitration, and conflict prevention, management and peace operations support to private individuals, governments, international organisations, multinational corporations, and governmental and non-governmental organisations.

YSC is a top-tier consulting firm in research, business seminars, publications, public policy and legal advice, offering a team approach built on multidisciplinary, specialised experience in law, corporate operations, conflict resolution, peacekeeping and peacemaking, capacity building, and speech writing. We are renowned for balanced, high-integrity service — organised, equipped and ready to deliver within hours of your order, available 24/7.

Lead Consultant

Speaking at a UN forum
Prof. Babafemi Adesina Badejo, Ph.D.
Founder, CEO & Lead Consultant

Prof. Babafemi A. Badejo is Professor of Political Science and International Relations at Chrisland University, Abeokuta, Nigeria, and the Founder, CEO and Lead Consultant of Yintab Strategy Consults. He brings almost 24 years of service with the United Nations and the African Union, including postings in Somalia, Liberia, Guinea-Bissau and Darfur, Sudan.

He served as Deputy Special Representative of the UN Secretary-General for Somalia and, most recently, as Head of the Political Affairs Section, UN-AU Mission in Darfur (UNAMID). He holds a Ph.D. in Political Science from UCLA and an LL.B. from the University of Lagos, and is a Solicitor and Advocate of the Supreme Court of Nigeria, an Associate of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (UK), and a member of the Lagos Multi-Door Courthouse Panel of Neutrals.

An author of over 50 academic publications and 150 opinion pieces, he is the recipient of the 2025 Nelson Mandela Distinguished Africanist Award and Djibouti's National Order of 27 June 1977, for his sustained support of the Somali peace process.
